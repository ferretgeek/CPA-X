# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

本项目致力于建设友善、尊重、包容且专业的开源社区。所有参与者均应遵守以下准则。

CPA-X is committed to a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and professional open-source community.

## Our pledge / 我们的承诺

Everyone participating in good faith receives the same opportunity to contribute, regardless of background, identity, experience, language, ability, or personal characteristics. We will communicate with empathy, honesty, and respect.

## Encouraged behavior / 鼓励的行为

- Respect the project's purpose and other contributors' time.
- Give and receive constructive technical feedback gracefully.
- Acknowledge mistakes, take responsibility, and repair harm where possible.
- Credit sources and the work of others.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or deliberate intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or repeatedly ignoring clearly stated boundaries.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, spam, or misleading promotional content.
- Any conduct that reasonably threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Scope / 适用范围

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, releases, and other spaces where someone represents the CPA-X community.

## Reporting and enforcement / 举报与执行

Report a possible violation privately to the repository owner through the contact method shown on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ferretgeek). Do not publish personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity and repeated behavior. Reports will be handled as privately and consistently as practical.
